# Ecigmania Design System

## Product

Ecigmania is an affiliate-commerce platform focused on helping
customers discover products and visit the merchant through an
affiliate link.

---

## Design Personality

Ecigmania should feel:

- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Clean
- Product-focused
- Curated

It should NOT feel:

- Cheap
- Overly promotional
- Generic e-commerce
- Like an AI-generated dashboard
- Visually noisy

---

## Customer Experience

The customer interface should prioritize:

1. Product discovery
2. Product image
3. Product title
4. Price
5. Rating
6. Affiliate action

Customers should be able to scan products quickly.

---

## Admin Experience

The admin interface should feel:

- Professional
- Efficient
- Structured
- Information-dense
- Operational

Customer-facing UI and admin UI should share the same visual
language but may use different layouts and density.

---

## Typography

Primary font:
Inter or equivalent modern sans-serif.

Weights:
- Regular: 400
- Medium: 500
- Semi-bold: 600
- Bold: 700 only when necessary

Avoid excessive font-weight variation.

---

## Color Direction

Base background:
#F7F7F5

Surface:
#FFFFFF

Primary text:
#171717

Secondary text:
#737373

Border:
#E5E5E5

Primary action:
#171717

Primary action hover:
#2B2B2B

Success:
#15803D

Rating:
#F59E0B

Avoid excessive use of colors.

Do not use gradients as a primary visual treatment.

---

## Shape

Product cards:
10px–12px radius

Buttons:
6px–8px radius

Inputs:
8px radius

Avoid excessive pill-shaped components.

Avoid huge rounded containers.

---

48px–64px

---

## Product Cards

Structure:

1. Product image
2. Brand
3. Product title
4. Rating
5. Price
6. Affiliate CTA

Product cards should be left-aligned.

Images should have a consistent aspect ratio.

Cards use a subtle border.

Default state:
1px solid border

Avoid heavy shadows.

Hover state may use a very subtle shadow or small
vertical movement.

---

## Affiliate CTA

Preferred copy:

"View Offer"
"Check Price"
"Shop at Merchant"

Avoid:

"BUY NOW!"
"GET IT NOW!"
or other aggressive language.

Use an external-link or arrow-up-right icon when appropriate.

The CTA should clearly communicate that the user
will leave Ecigmania.

---

## Product Listing

Desktop:

- Max width around 1280px
- Filter sidebar
- 3–4 column product grid

Tablet:

- Collapsible filter
- 3 column grid

Mobile:

- Filter button
- 2 column product grid

---

## Visual Rules

Avoid:

- Generic AI dashboard layouts
- Excessive gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Huge shadows
- Excessive rounded cards
- Center-aligned product information
- Excessive animations
- Random accent colors
- Decorative UI without functional purpose

Prefer:

- Strong typography hierarchy
- Consistent spacing
- High-quality product imagery
- Clear CTAs
- Subtle borders
- Restrained colors
- Intentional whitespace
- Consistent component reuse
